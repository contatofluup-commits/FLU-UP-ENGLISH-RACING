The story

A song about being young in a scary time

1984Year of the song
MünsterAlphaville's city, in Germany
Synth-popPop music made with synthesizers
Marian GoldThe singer

In 1984 the world was in the Cold War. The USA and the Soviet Union had thousands of nuclear bombs, and many young people were afraid that a war would end everything. In "Forever Young", the singer is young and wants to enjoy life: dance, play music, follow his dreams. But he also asks a big question: do you really want to live forever?

The song is happy and sad at the same time. We call this feeling bittersweet or nostalgic.

Em português

Em 1984 o mundo vivia a Guerra Fria. EUA e União Soviética tinham milhares de bombas nucleares, e muitos jovens tinham medo de uma guerra acabar com tudo. Na música, o cantor quer aproveitar a juventude (dançar, tocar, sonhar), mas também pergunta: "você quer mesmo viver para sempre?". É uma música alegre e triste ao mesmo tempo: bittersweet (agridoce).

Pronunciation

Sounds of the song

gonna = going to

"Are you gonna drop the bomb?" Informal, used in songs and speech. In a test, write going to.

wanna = want to

You'll hear it in many songs. "I wanna be young." Again: speak it, but don't write it in formal English.

we'll = we will

"We'll let them come true." Say it like "weel", one short sound.

Em português

Gonna e wanna são formas faladas de going to e want to. Aparecem muito em músicas, mas na escrita formal (e nas provas) use a forma completa. Cante junto com o refrão para treinar o ritmo.

Our unit

Two more futures: in progress or finished?

Future continuouswill be + -ing
NOWtomorrow 8 p.m.
Future perfectwill have + participle

will be + -ing

An action in progress at a moment in the future.

  • Tomorrow at 8 p.m., I will be dancing in style.
  • She won't be sleeping at midnight.
  • Will you be watching the skies tonight?
will have + participle

An action finished before a moment in the future (by…, by the time…).

  • By Friday, we will have learned the chorus.
  • He won't have forgotten the song by then.
  • Will our dreams have come true by 2030?
Em português

Will be + -ing (future continuous): uma ação que vai estar acontecendo num momento do futuro. "Amanhã às 8 eu vou estar dançando."

Will have + particípio (future perfect): uma ação que já vai ter terminado antes de um momento do futuro. "Até sexta, a gente já vai ter aprendido o refrão." Palavras-chave: by, by the time, by then.
